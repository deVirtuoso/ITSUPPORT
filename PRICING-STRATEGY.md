# NEXUSDESK pricing strategy

Goal: make at least £10,000 per month with the best balance of profit and delivery effort.

## Recommended model

The flagship offer is **Company Unlimited at £2,000 per company per month**. Five subscribed companies create **£10,000 monthly recurring revenue** before Stripe fees, software, hosting, staffing, tax, and other operating costs.

One-off sessions should stay available, but they should not be the main growth engine:

| Plan | Price | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Standard | £99 for up to 60 minutes | Entry-level paid support and lead qualification. |
| Priority | £179 for up to 2 hours | Better margin for complex issues. |
| Urgent | £299 for up to 4 hours | High-value emergency work and price anchor. |
| Company Unlimited | £2,000/month | Main recurring revenue product. |

## Profit guardrails

- Sell Company Unlimited as unlimited online support for one company, not unlimited projects.
- Keep exclusions visible: on-site work, hardware repair, managed hosting, custom development, full website builds, bulk migrations, and third-party fees.
- Verify subscribed company domains and approved contacts so one subscription does not become support for multiple unrelated businesses.
- Track ticket volume per company. If a subscriber regularly requires project-level work, quote a separate project or managed services agreement.
- Use one-off sessions as a conversion path into Company Unlimited: after a second urgent or priority session, offer the monthly subscription.

## Monthly target math

- 5 Company Unlimited subscriptions = £10,000/month recurring revenue.
- 4 subscriptions + 7 urgent sessions = £10,093/month.
- 3 subscriptions + 12 priority sessions + 7 urgent sessions = £10,241/month.

The cleanest target is five company subscriptions. The one-off plans are useful for cash flow, but the subscription reduces monthly sales pressure and makes staffing easier to plan.
