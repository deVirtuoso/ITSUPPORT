FAQ

Common questions

Why pay before any remote connection?

It prevents abuse, reduces fraudulent access attempts, and lets us prioritize paying customers with adequate staffing. You still get a clear receipt from Stripe and defined next steps after payment.

What if you can’t fix my issue?

Many desktop, software, and website problems are fixable remotely; some are not. We’ll outline what we tried and realistic options (escalation, reinstall, host escalation, hardware service). Credits and refunds follow our Refund policy.

Do you support phones or tablets?

Our core scope is laptops, desktops, and websites. For phones or tablets, reach us through Contact before booking so we can confirm fit.

What website work is included?

We help with configuration, CMS and plugin issues, DNS and SSL basics, performance triage, and hosting dashboards you already control. We do not guarantee recovery from major host outages, build new sites from scratch in a single session, or take over enterprise-scale infrastructure unless separately agreed.

What does unlimited company support mean?

Company Unlimited covers unlimited online support requests for one subscribed company and its staff while the subscription is active. It covers normal troubleshooting and configuration within our remote scope, not custom builds, new website projects, on-site work, hardware repair, managed hosting, or abusive/high-volume automation.

Is my data safe?

We use reputable remote tools and recommend you close sensitive documents before sharing your screen. We do not request passwords by email and we discourage sharing them in chat—use secure methods your technician provides.
