A process built for quality and accountability

Payment first. Then we open the session.

Remote support is powerful—and easy to misuse. We require Stripe checkout before any screen sharing or remote control begins. That keeps our engineers focused on real customers and protects everyone’s time.

  1. 01

    Pick your plan

    Select the session length and priority that match your issue. You’ll see the total upfront—no hidden fees.

  2. 02

    Pay securely with Stripe

    Complete checkout on Stripe’s PCI-compliant page. We never store your full card details. Your payment confirms your place in line.

  3. 03

    Verify & schedule

    After payment, you receive a receipt and instructions to join a secure support channel. Identity and issue details are confirmed before tools are used.

  4. 04

    Remote session & resolution

    With your consent, we connect via approved remote software. We document actions taken and next steps if follow-up is needed.

Why we don’t offer “try before you pay”

Unpaid triage invites spam, no-shows, and security risk. Requiring payment first lets us staff real specialists around the clock and keep sessions short, honest, and effective—for you and for our team.

Trust & safety

Professional, bounded, and transparent

  • Stripe handles payments. Card data stays on Stripe’s infrastructure; we receive only what’s needed to fulfill the service.
  • Least-privilege remote access. You approve each connection. We explain actions before system-level changes when possible.
  • Clear scope. Software support for consumer and small-business devices—not managed enterprise SLAs unless separately contracted.
  • Honest limits. Some problems need a shop or manufacturer RMA. We’ll say so early, not after burning your session.

Before you pay

Have your device charged, administrator access ready if required, and a stable internet connection. You’ll need to install or allow only the remote tool we specify—never unsolicited downloads.

Support queue is monitored 24/7. Session start times depend on plan and volume. We do not provide on-site or hardware bench service.

FAQ

Common questions

Why pay before any remote connection?

It prevents abuse, reduces fraudulent access attempts, and lets us prioritize paying customers with adequate staffing. You still get a clear receipt from Stripe and defined next steps after payment.

What if you can’t fix my issue?

Many software problems are fixable remotely; some are not. We’ll outline what we tried and realistic options (escalation, reinstall, hardware service). Credits and refunds follow our Refund policy.

Do you support phones or tablets?

This page focuses on laptop and desktop computers. For other device types, reach us through Contact before booking.

Is my data safe?

We use reputable remote tools and recommend you close sensitive documents before sharing your screen. We do not request passwords by email and we discourage sharing them in chat—use secure methods your technician provides.
